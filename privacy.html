Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Beacon for MeshCore ("Beacon") is an iPhone app that connects to your own MeshCore LoRa radio so you can share your location, safety status and messages with a group of people you choose.

What we collect

Nothing. Beacon has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no advertising. The developer does not receive, store or have access to any of your data.

What the app uses, and where it goes

  • Location. When you choose a group channel, Beacon sends your position from your iPhone to your own radio over Bluetooth. Your radio broadcasts it over the mesh to the members of that channel. It is never sent to the Public channel, and never to the developer. If you allow it, sharing continues while your phone is locked, and iOS shows the location indicator.
  • Messages and status updates. Chats, safety status, the emergency state and the rally point travel the same way: phone → your radio → the people in your channel or the person you message.
  • Bluetooth. Used only to connect to your MeshCore radio.
  • Camera. Used only to scan a group's QR code when you join a group. Images are read on your iPhone and never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Notifications. Shown on your device for messages and status changes. They are created on your phone, with no push server.
  • Addresses. To show a street address for a member's position, Beacon asks Apple's MapKit service, which works under Apple's privacy policy. Addresses already looked up are saved on your device.

Data stored on your device

Beacon saves recent chats, the shared fields, contacts and settings on your iPhone so they're available offline. Deleting the app deletes this data. Settings you change in Radio admin are stored on your radio.

Who can see what you share

Anyone with the same channel name and secret on their radio can read what is sent on that channel. Keep your channel secret private, and share it only with people you trust. MeshCore radios encrypt channel traffic; the Public channel is readable by anyone nearby, so Beacon never shares location or status there.

Children

Beacon is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
